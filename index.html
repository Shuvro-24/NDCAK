About the Association

A Community Beyond Campus

Notre Dame College Association of KUET is a platform that brings together students and alumni of Notre Dame College currently studying or associated with Khulna University of Engineering & Technology.

The association aims to strengthen friendship, professional networking, leadership and collaboration among its members while preserving the values and traditions of Notre Dame College.

Our Mission

To build a supportive and connected NDC community within KUET.

Our Vision

To create a responsible, united and impactful alumni-student network.
